## Highlights ⭐️

* **In lockstep with passmcp 0.0.10**, the family's tenth release: passmcp-reporting is required at v0.0.10, and passmcp's trace runs at v0.0.10. The commands, the query language, the policy file, the dashboard, the graph format and the exit statuses are unchanged from 0.0.9.
* **15-repository family table in the README**: adds the six Apache-2.0 MCP language SDKs (`passmcp-sdk-go`, `passmcp-sdk-python`, `passmcp-sdk-java`, `passmcp-sdk-cpp`, `passmcp-sdk-typescript`, and `passmcp-sdk-rust`) alongside the existing nine family repositories at `0.0.10`.
